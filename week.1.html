Week 1 - Retrospective Questions and Answers

What went well

in the design process i was able to somewhat establish a baseline of what steps im going to take in my design processes for “New Enterprise” and different stages. From there i set up pages on Figjam and set up different sections for the stages like research, ux design, branding, ux refinements and other task i need complete in the processes. i was able to somewhat use last years processes or my work and apply it to this brief. Overall i didn't accomplish my sprint goal “Research and Develop into Problem Statements” as i still don't have a direction for my “Problem Statement”. But i do have a direction im going in for work i just need to apply myself more.

What didn’t go well

i wasn't able to do a sufficient amount research as i was set behind by health issues and struggling to find a specific direction to go in for the “Your designed solution must relate to an identified present day, real world problem.”. I did do a small brainstorm on some base level issues and problems people deal with in New Zealand/Local.

What can be improved

Ive learnt that i need to spread out my effort in-terms of where im putting it towards. As i spent more time on “my blog” and making it look nice rather on my formative “bootcamp - cupcake site”. So with that i need to spread effort out more evenly and or on the tasks that are due sooner. Another lesson ive learnt is although i organised what steps/sections/stages im planning to have there aren't set in stone as from seeing past year examples i realised that there are so many different directions it can go in for in-terms of it being a product, service or branch of UX content.

What actions will I take for the next sprint

actions i plan to take are spreading my time more evenly on tasks, leave room for changes/other directions in my design processes and keep on track with tasks and keep moving on to the next as i can always come back.
